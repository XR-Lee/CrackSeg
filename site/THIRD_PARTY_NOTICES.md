# Project-page asset notices

This project page is an independent implementation. It does not copy the HTML,
CSS, JavaScript, or paper assets of the MapGR reference page.

The scientific figures in `assets/images/` are derived from the authors' own
paper, **3D Modeling and Automated Measurement of Concrete Cracks via Segment
Anything Refinement and Visual Inertial LiDAR Fusion**, arXiv:2501.09203v2, and
from the companion CrackSeg demo bundle. They remain subject to the paper and
dataset distribution terms. The homepage walkway image is a lossless crop
of the colorized semantic slab in reconstruction-results.png (panel b).

The CrackSeg source repository is licensed separately; see the repository's
top-level `LICENSE` and the notices attached to vendored third-party components.

The scientific figures in `crack-evolution/assets/` are from the authors’
preprint **Cross-View and Cross-Time Tracking of Crack Evolution in Concrete**,
arXiv:2610.33550v1. The two homepage tracking panels are drawn from Figure 9(b).
