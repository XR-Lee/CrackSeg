Detect cracks and measure their width in 3D

Crack-aware prompts guide SAM refinement of the initial segmentation. Image and LiDAR fusion places those crack regions in a metric 3D point cloud, where the method can localize cracks and automatically measure their width.

Track the same crack and trace its new growth

Align adjacent observations, use crack shape to separate crossing branches and reconnect compatible fragments, and assign newly visible extension to each tracked crack. Growth length is evaluated in image coordinates.
